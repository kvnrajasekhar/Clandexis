# Clandexis Launch Video Plan (brag-slim)

## Angle
Most Indian MSMEs do not use ERP software because traditional ERPs require desktop computers, accounting degrees, and complex data entry. Instead, merchants run their businesses over WhatsApp. Clandexis turns that informal WhatsApp chat into an invisible, auditable ERP powered by Google Gemini and PostgreSQL.

## The Hook (0.0s – 3.5s)
A bold, counter-intuitive premise:
"Most MSMEs don't use ERPs. They run on WhatsApp."
Cut to: "Meet Clandexis. The Invisible ERP."

## Highlights
1. **Vernacular WhatsApp Audio & Text:** A merchant types naturally in Hinglish:
   `"Ramesh ko 10 cotton shirt bechi ₹2000 ki, ₹500 cash diya baaki udhaar"`
2. **Gemini Schema 1.1 + Zero Hallucinations:** Stage 4 Business Validator enforces financial math and generates an interactive 1-tap confirmation card `[✅ Confirm (हाँ)] [❌ Cancel (रद्द)]`.
3. **Atomic Ledger Commit & Instant PDF Invoicing:** Clicking Confirm instantly posts the double-entry journal entry, updates customer udhaar, deducts inventory, and auto-generates a PDF invoice.
4. **Live Merchant / Judge Dashboard:** Real-time visibility into Today's Sales, Cash/UPI Collected, Outstanding Udhaar Credit, and Stock movement.

## The Punchline (16.5s – 20.0s)
"No apps to download. No accounting courses. Just WhatsApp."
"Clandexis | Google Gemini + PostgreSQL"

## Tone & Visual Identity
- **Tone:** `default` (Punchy, slick, modern product launch).
- **Format:** Landscape (1920×1080), 30 fps, 20.0 seconds (600 frames).
- **Palette:**
  - Background: Deep Obsidian Slate (`#090d16` to `#0f172a`)
  - Accent / Primary: Emerald Green (`#10b981` / `#059669` WhatsApp green)
  - Secondary: Electric Blue / Violet (`#3b82f6` / `#6366f1` Google Gemini AI glow)
  - Text: Crisp White (`#f8fafc`) and Muted Slate (`#94a3b8`)
  - Warning/Credit: Amber (`#f59e0b`)
- **Typography:** Inter / SF Pro / Helvetica Neue with native Devanagari typography.
